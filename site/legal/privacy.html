Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no forms, no account.
  • Fonts, icons and videos are served from wmux.org itself, including on the docs and orchestrator pages. No page loads anything from third parties.
  • Your language choice is kept in your browser under wmux-lang (wmux-orch-lang on the orchestrator page). It never leaves your device.
  • The orchestrator page also remembers, for the current tab only, that you have seen its hint (wmux-hint-seen).

Hosting logs

Netlify, the host, processes technical request data such as IP address, browser and requested page to serve and protect the site. See Netlify's privacy policy.

Links and downloads

Downloads, source code, issues and discussions live on GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

The wmux app

  • No telemetry, no analytics, no crash upload, no account.
  • On its own, it checks GitHub for updates: 5 seconds after launch, then every 6 hours. Set WMUX_DISABLE_UPDATER=1 to turn it off.
  • Opening the release notes fetches them from the GitHub API.
  • Everything else happens only when you ask: pages opened in the browser panel, SSH connections, the Remote Console (off by default) and ChatGPT Web dictation (opt-in).
  • Settings, sessions and logs stay in %APPDATA%\wmux. Saved terminal output is opt-in, local and unencrypted.
  • Crash reports are created locally with wmux crash-report and shared only if you choose to.

Your rights

wmux.org collects no personal data of its own. For questions, open a GitHub discussion. For hosting logs, contact Netlify.
